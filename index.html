Operational continuity

Follow perishable ingredients through supplier pickup, receiving, cold storage, processing, and grocery delivery. Record ETAs, product value, spoilage, fallback capacity, and remediation.

Inbound, processing & outbound tasks

Physical issues & remediation

Track a real-world consequence and who is addressing it.

Operational learning: Supplier and transport systems coordinate ingredient orders; receiving records lot and quantity; temperature-controlled storage protects perishable stock; processing converts inputs into batches; outbound dispatch delivers finished products to grocery stores. Incorrect lot, inventory, temperature, or dispatch data can cause spoilage, rework, delayed production, or missed deliveries. Demo records are illustrative, not live sensors or food-safety advice.
